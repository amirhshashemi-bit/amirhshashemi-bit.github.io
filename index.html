ELOHIM

AI education, made clear.

ELOHIM is an experimental project intended to help its owner research topics in artificial intelligence and prepare original educational video materials for a YouTube channel. Research may inform topic selection; published material should be independently written and reviewed.

How account access is used

If the owner connects a YouTube account, ELOHIM may access channel information and, after the owner configures and authorizes the relevant workflow, manage channel resources for the owner’s requested purposes. Access is not sold or shared for advertising. Public uploads and other consequential channel changes require the owner’s approval.
