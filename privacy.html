Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

ELOHIM is an experimental, owner-operated project intended to assist with researching and preparing educational AI content for YouTube.

Information accessed

When the owner authorizes Google/YouTube access, the application may receive account and channel information, channel metadata, and other YouTube data permitted by the scopes shown on Google’s consent screen. The actual access depends on the owner’s configuration and authorization.

Use of information

Data is used only to provide the owner-requested channel checks and, if separately implemented and authorized, channel-management and content-publishing functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their independent purposes. Content research should be used to create original material rather than copy another creator’s work.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored in private local configuration or restricted secret storage for automation. They must not be placed in this public website or a public source repository. The project’s owner is responsible for access control, retention, and deletion of stored credentials and generated working files.

Sharing

Information is not shared except as necessary to call Google/YouTube APIs at the owner’s direction, or when required by law. Google’s handling of data is governed by Google’s own policies and terms.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the application’s access through their Google Account security settings. Stored project data and credentials should be deleted from the systems where they were saved when no longer needed. This experimental project does not currently provide a public self-service deletion interface.

Children

This project is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the project’s data practices change. For privacy questions, contact amirhashemizadeh82@gmail.com.
